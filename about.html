Our Story

Built For
The Next
Generation.

Eagle Sound Studios was founded in Lusaka, Zambia, with a simple goal: to give the next generation of African artists a studio that matches their ambition. Not just gear, a real creative home.

We saw too many talented artists forced to choose between quality and cost, between professional sound and keeping their own identity. We built Eagle Sound to eliminate that choice.

Today, we work with artists, musicians, producers, choirs, voice over artists, and creators from across Zambia and beyond. Every project gets the same thing: our full attention and the best sound we can help make.

We're not just a studio. We're a launchpad.
