04 / FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

How much does membership cost? +

Membership is free to join. You only pay for the sessions you book. Benefits like half price Mondays, the 3rd session free, and the birthday session apply automatically once you're a member.

When does my membership start? +

Your membership starts the moment you sign up. All perks activate as soon as you book your first session.

Do I need to be a signed artist? +

Not at all. The membership is open to everyone, independent artists, producers, choirs, voice over artists, podcasters, and anyone who uses the studio. Everyone gets the same benefits.

How does the 3rd session free work? +

Book and pay for two full price sessions. Your third session is completely free. There's no time limit, take as long as you need between sessions.

How do I claim my free birthday session? +

Make sure your birthday is on file when you sign up. During your birthday week, book a session and let us know, the 2 hours are on us. You can bring one friend, and they'll get 10% off their next session.

Can I share my membership with someone else? +

Membership is per person. However, you can bring guests to your sessions and refer friends to earn referral rewards for both of you.

What if I move away or stop using the studio? +

There's no commitment and no cancellation fee. Your membership stays active as long as you'd like, even if you take a break. Come back anytime.
