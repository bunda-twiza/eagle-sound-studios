05 / FAQ

Common
Questions.

What should I bring to a recording session? +

Bring your instrument if you have one, your lyrics or notes, water, and anything that helps you get in the zone. We provide the gear, the room, and the engineer. If you're unsure, just ask, we'll tell you exactly what to bring.

Can I bring my own producer or engineer? +

Yes. You're welcome to bring your own team. We'll provide the room and gear, and stay out of the way unless you need us. If you want our engineer on the session too, let us know when you book.

Do you work with first time artists? +

Absolutely. Some of our best sessions have been with artists recording for the very first time. We'll walk you through the process, explain everything, and make sure you're comfortable before we hit record.

How long does a session usually take? +

It depends on the project. A single vocal recording can take 1-2 hours. A full song with multiple instruments and vocals can take a full day. We'll help you plan the right amount of time when you book.

Can I get my files after the session? +

Yes. You'll get all your raw recordings, mixed files, and final masters. We archive everything for a year, so even if you forget something, we've got you covered.

Do you offer packages for multiple songs? +

Yes. If you're working on an EP or album, we offer discounted packages. Book a consultation and we'll build a plan that works for your project and your budget.

Can I visit the studio before booking? +

Of course. We encourage it. Come see the space, meet the team, and get a feel for how we work. Book a tour by contacting us directly.
